Privacy Policy

Murder Notebook: Whodunit

Last updated: October 30, 2026 · Türkçe

In short: Murder Notebook does not ask for an account, name or email, does not send your game data to any server and does not track you. Ads in the app are shown by Google AdMob and are not personalized to you.

Stored on your device

Your solved cases, stars, daily streak, the grid marks of unfinished cases and your settings are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to us or anyone else. They are deleted when you delete the app or tap Settings → “Reset progress”.

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notifications are scheduled on your phone; they are not sent from a server. To pick the reminder time, the hours at which you usually open the app are also kept on your phone only. You can turn the reminder off in Settings at any time.

Cases and language

Cases are generated on your phone itself; the app does not connect to the internet for this. On first launch, the app reads your phone’s language setting to choose between English and Turkish; this is not sent anywhere. You can change the language in Settings → “Language”.

Ads

The small ad banner at the bottom of the screen and the optional rewarded ads you can watch for a hint are shown by Google AdMob. Ads are not personalized: they are not chosen by your interests, you are not asked for tracking permission and the advertising identifier on Apple devices is not accessed. To show ads, count impressions and prevent fraud, Google processes limited data such as IP address, device and app information, ad interactions and error information; this is not used to identify you or to track you across other apps. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the consent required by law can be changed in Settings → “Ad privacy choices”. Details: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

There are no interstitial ads; a rewarded ad only plays when you tap Hint → “Watch an ad”. No ads are shown on the welcome screen or in the training case. We do not have access to ad data; we only see total impression and revenue reports.

Links

The “Support” and “Privacy” links in Settings open this website in your browser. The site uses no cookies or tracking tools; it is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Children

The game consists of murder puzzles told with humor and without any images of violence; it is not designed for children. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (PG).

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.
