Murder Notebook

Who, with what, where? Murder puzzles solved with logic

Türkçe

In Murder Notebook, every case is a small logic puzzle. Meet the suspects, the weapons and the places, read the clues and fill in the detective grid to find the killer, the murder weapon and the scene of the crime. Every day brings a new case, the same for everyone.

FAQ

How do I fill in the grid?

Tap a square once for ✕ (these two can’t belong to the same person), twice for ● (they belong to the same person); a third tap clears it. A long press places a ● directly. The “How to play” tab in the app has a worked example.

I’m stuck. Is this case unsolvable?

No. Every case is generated so that the clues and the grid’s logic lead to exactly one solution without guessing, and this is checked before a case ever reaches the app. When you are stuck, the “Hint” button shows your wrong marks or points to the clue that tells the grid something new right now.

How are stars counted?

Every case starts with three stars, and each wrong accusation costs one; you always keep at least one star for a solved case. If you are stuck, you can get a hint: watch a short ad (no star lost) or pay one star. A hint opens one square of the grid and locks it. If you give up and see the solution, the case closes without stars, but the next case still opens.

My daily streak broke.

The streak counts the days in a row on which you solved the case of the day on its own day. You can still solve the cases of past days later, but they don’t count toward the streak.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no account, email or password. Cases are generated on your phone, so the game works offline (the ad banner is hidden then). Your progress is stored only on your phone.

Why are there ads?

To keep the game free, there is a small ad banner at the bottom of the screen. There are no interstitial ads; a full-screen ad only plays when you choose to watch one for a hint.

Can I play in Turkish?

Yes. The game starts in your phone’s language; you can switch between English and Türkçe in Settings → “Language”. The cases are the same in both languages.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Not yet; progress is kept only on the phone you play on.
